Clinical Trust Framework

How MedicalFund Ensures 100% Trust

Unlike general crowdfunding sites, medical cases require clinical scrutiny. Here is how your money safely saves lives.

Step 01

Document & Hospital Audit

Our medical board contacts the treating hospital to verify estimate letters, diagnostic reports, and patient bed admission details.

Step 02

Direct Hospital Payout

Funds are transferred directly into the hospital's verified account against bills—preventing diversion or misuse of donors' funds.

Step 03

Audited Receipts & Updates

Every donor receives an instant tax-deductible receipt (80G / 501c3) along with clinical progress notifications during recovery.
